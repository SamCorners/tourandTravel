Best Time to Visit Sikkim and Darjeeling: A Month-by-Month Guide

When to go for snow, for flowers and for clear Kanchenjunga views, how holiday seasons affect crowds and prices, and when to avoid the monsoon.

By the Sikkim Tour and Travel team · Updated 12 October 2026

The honest answer to "when is the best time to visit Sikkim?" is: it depends on what you want to see. Snow, flowers and clear mountain views each have their own season, and they do not fully overlap. Below we map each season to what you will see, and to the holiday periods when hotels fill up.

The short answer

  • Clearest Himalayan views: October to early December.
  • Flowers and pleasant weather: March to May (rhododendrons peak in April).
  • Snow: December to March at Tsomgo Lake, Nathula, Lachung and Zero Point; snow in Gangtok town itself is rare.
  • Avoid if you can: mid-June to September, when monsoon rain causes landslides and closes roads, especially in North Sikkim.

Temperatures are rough day ranges in Gangtok (about 5,400 ft). Darjeeling (6,700 ft) runs a couple of degrees colder. Lachung, Zuluk and Tsomgo are far colder – often below zero at night from November to March.

Holiday seasons and crowds

Most families plan around one of these windows, so expect higher demand and prices during them:

April–May summer vacation

The busiest time of the year. Hotels in Gangtok and Darjeeling fill up, and toy train tickets sell out. The weather is lovely, and a welcome break from summer heat on the plains. Book 2 to 3 months ahead, especially for the last two weeks of May. Early June is still fine before the monsoon sets in.

Dasara, Puja and Deepavali holidays (October–November)

The best weather for views. Durga Puja also brings heavy domestic tourism from West Bengal, so the week around Puja is crowded. If you can travel just after it, you get the best of both.

Christmas, New Year and January holidays

For families who want snow and do not mind the cold. Tsomgo Lake is usually frozen. Be ready for a backup day in case heavy snowfall closes a road. Pack seriously warm clothes: thermals, a padded jacket, gloves and a cap. Christmas–New Year week is the most expensive of the year.

Best time for specific places

Tsomgo Lake and Nathula

Snow from about December to March or early April. Nathula is open to Indian tourists on Wednesday to Sunday only and closes during heavy snow. In summer, Tsomgo is a clear blue lake with snow on the surrounding peaks.

Yumthang Valley and Lachung

April and May for the famous rhododendron and primula blooms. December to March for snow, though roads can close. Avoid the monsoon months.

Gurudongmar Lake and Lachen

Traditionally October to May, with the lake frozen in winter. Since the 2023 Teesta floods and later landslides, access to Lachen and Gurudongmar has depended heavily on road repairs and government permission. Check with us for current status before planning around it.

Darjeeling and Tiger Hill

October to early December for clear sunrise views. March to May is pleasant but often hazy by late morning.

Silk Route and Zuluk

October to December for the clearest skies from Thambi View Point; March to May for rhododendrons. In January and February, snow can block the high road near Nathang and Kupup.

What about the monsoon?

Some travellers like the green, empty hills of July and August, and hotel prices drop. But we will be straight with you: landslides are common, North Sikkim often closes, flights into Bagdogra can be delayed, and you may not see the mountains at all. If you have flexible dates, choose another season. If you must go in the monsoon, stick to Gangtok and Darjeeling and keep a buffer day before your return flight.

Our recommendation

For first-time visitors, October–November or late March–April give the best balance of weather, views and crowds. For snow with small children, aim for late December to February and keep the trip to Gangtok and Darjeeling. Honeymooners who want snow and flowers both can try late March or early April. Quick answers

Which month is best for snow in Sikkim?

December to March. Tsomgo Lake, Nathula, Lachung and Zero Point usually have snow in this period. Snow in Gangtok town itself is rare.

Is May a good time to visit Sikkim?

Yes. May is pleasant (about 14–22°C in Gangtok) and Yumthang is in flower. It is also peak season, so book 2–3 months ahead.

When are the clearest views of Kanchenjunga?

October to early December, especially in the mornings. Spring views are often hazy by late morning.
