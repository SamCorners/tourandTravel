Before you book

The questions travellers ask us first

Straight answers to what most people want to know before they call.

Will we get vegetarian or Jain food?

Yes. Gangtok and Darjeeling have restaurants serving idli, dosa and thalis, and our hotels make curd rice, upma or dal-rice on request. Jain and no onion-garlic meals are arranged in advance.

Food guide

It's so far – how do we get there?

Fly to Bagdogra (IXB) or take a train to New Jalpaiguri (NJP) – the two gateways for Sikkim and Darjeeling. Our driver meets you at either point, and pickup and drop are free with every package.

How to reach

Is the cold OK for our parents and kids?

Gangtok and Darjeeling are comfortable for most families with a good jacket and thermals. We keep high places like Tsomgo to short day visits and plan rest after long drives.

Weather by month

Who do we call if something goes wrong?

One coordinator looks after your trip from pickup to drop and is on call 24x7 by phone and WhatsApp – for road updates, hotel requests, or help with a doctor or pharmacy.

Our booking policy

Do we need permits?

For Tsomgo Lake, Nathula, North Sikkim and the Silk Route, yes. Send us Aadhaar or voter ID copies and passport photos on WhatsApp – we apply for everything.

Permit guide

Altitude sickness at Nathula or Gurudongmar?

We always spend a night in Gangtok before going higher, keep visits short, and tell you honestly who should skip Gurudongmar (small children, heart or lung patients).

When to go

Best time to visit Sikkim

October–November for the clearest views of Kanchenjunga. March–May for flowers, pleasant days and a break from summer heat on the plains. December–March if your children want to see snow at Tsomgo Lake and Lachung. We suggest avoiding mid-June to September, when monsoon landslides close roads.

Sikkim tour questions, answered

How many days are enough for a Sikkim trip?

We suggest at least 5 nights / 6 days, and ideally 6 nights / 7 days if you are travelling from far away. The first and last days go in travel, so this gives you four to five full days in the hills without rushing.

Is the pickup from Bagdogra airport included?

Yes. Every package includes free pickup from Bagdogra airport (IXB) or New Jalpaiguri station (NJP) and drop back on the last day, in a private vehicle.

Do you book flights too?

Our packages cover everything from Bagdogra / NJP onwards. We are happy to suggest flight timings that fit the itinerary; you can book flights yourself at the best fare you find.

How much advance do we pay to confirm?

We share the exact payment schedule with your written quote. Nothing is payable to receive an itinerary or price.

Is Sikkim safe for families and senior citizens?

Sikkim is one of the safest and cleanest states in India for tourists. The main things to plan for are mountain roads, weather and altitude – which is exactly what our itineraries are designed around.

What is the best Sikkim family tour package for first-timers?

The Gangtok & Darjeeling 5N/6D tour, or the Darjeeling–Pelling–Gangtok 6N/7D tour if you have an extra day. Both are easy on children and grandparents.
