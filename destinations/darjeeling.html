Top things to do in Darjeeling

Tiger Hill sunrise

You leave the hotel around 4 am for the drive of about 11 km to Tiger Hill, where the first light turns Kanchenjunga – the third-highest mountain in the world – pink and gold. Clear mornings are most likely from October to early December. It is cold at the top in every season.

Toy train joy ride

The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway is part of the UNESCO World Heritage listing for the Mountain Railways of India. The joy ride runs from Darjeeling to Ghoom and back in about two hours, with a stop at Batasia Loop. Tickets sell out in peak season, so book ahead.

Batasia Loop and Ghoom Monastery

Both are on the way back from Tiger Hill. Batasia is a spiral of railway track with a war memorial and gardens; Ghoom is one of the oldest monasteries in the area.

Himalayan Mountaineering Institute and the zoo

The institute's museum tells the story of Everest expeditions and Tenzing Norgay. The Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park next door is known for red pandas and snow leopards. Both are closed on Thursdays.

Tea gardens, Peace Pagoda and the Mall

Visit a tea estate to see plucking and processing, stop at the Japanese Peace Pagoda, and spend the evening walking around Chowrasta and the Mall, where you can buy tea, shawls and woollens.

Darjeeling or Gangtok – or both?

Darjeeling has the tea gardens, the toy train and Tiger Hill; Gangtok has Tsomgo Lake, Nathula and access to North Sikkim. Most first-time visitors combine them – our Gangtok & Darjeeling 5N/6D tour is the most popular way to do it. Because Darjeeling is closer to Bagdogra, it is also a good first stop if your flight lands in the afternoon.

Getting to Darjeeling

Bagdogra airport (IXB) and New Jalpaiguri station (NJP) are both about three hours away by road, through the tea estates around Kurseong. We include pickup and drop in every package and offer standalone cabs. See how to reach Sikkim and Darjeeling.

Weather

Expect roughly 8–18°C in April–May, 5–15°C in October–November, and close to freezing on winter nights. Mist rolls in most afternoons. June to September is monsoon season, with heavy rain and few mountain views.

Darjeeling: quick answers

How many days are enough for Darjeeling?

Two nights covers Tiger Hill, the toy train and the main sights. Most visitors combine Darjeeling with two or three nights in Gangtok.

Which days are HMI and the zoo closed?

The Himalayan Mountaineering Institute and the zoo are closed on Thursdays.

Do Indians need a permit for Darjeeling?

No. Darjeeling is in West Bengal and needs no permit for Indian nationals.

Can you book the toy train for us?

Yes, we book joy ride tickets in advance where available. Tell us your dates early in peak season.
