Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1: Bagdogra / NJP to Kalimpong

    Pickup and a shorter first drive of about 3 hours to Kalimpong. Afternoon at Deolo Hill for the view over the Teesta valley, and a cactus nursery.

    Overnight KalimpongDinner~70 km · 3 hrs

  2. Day 2: Kalimpong to Gangtok

    Morning visit to Durpin Monastery and Dr Graham's Homes, then drive to Gangtok (about 3 hours). Evening on MG Marg.

    Overnight GangtokBreakfast, dinner~75 km · 3 hrs

  3. Day 3: Tsomgo Lake and Baba Mandir

    Day trip to Tsomgo Lake and Baba Mandir with permits arranged by us. Nathula Pass optional on Wednesday–Sunday. Afternoon rest; we collect documents for the North Sikkim permit.

    Overnight GangtokBreakfast, dinnerFull day

  4. Day 4: Gangtok to Lachung

    Drive north to Lachung (8,600 ft) via Seven Sisters Waterfall and Chungthang. Simple, warm hotel; dinner in.

    Overnight LachungBreakfast, lunch, dinner~115 km · 6 hrs

  5. Day 5: Yumthang Valley, back to Gangtok

    Morning at Yumthang Valley (11,800 ft) and the hot spring; Zero Point optional. Return to Gangtok in the evening.

    Overnight GangtokBreakfast, lunch, dinnerFull day · ~8 hrs

  6. Day 6: Gangtok to Pelling via Temi and Ravangla

    Short Gangtok stops (Tashi View Point, Enchey Monastery), then drive via the Temi Tea Garden to Ravangla Buddha Park and on to Pelling.

    Overnight PellingBreakfast, dinner~130 km · 6–7 hrs

  7. Day 7: Pelling sightseeing

    Pemayangtse Monastery, Rabdentse Ruins, Sanga Choeling skywalk, Khecheopalri Lake, Kanchenjunga Falls. Early night with the mountains outside the window.

    Overnight PellingBreakfast, dinnerLocal tour · 6 hrs

  8. Day 8: Pelling to Darjeeling via Namchi

    Drive to Namchi for Char Dham (Siddheshwar Dham) – replicas of the four dhams with a large Shiva statue – and Samdruptse, the giant Guru Padmasambhava statue. Continue to Darjeeling.

    Overnight DarjeelingBreakfast, dinner~140 km · 7 hrs

  9. Day 9: Tiger Hill and Darjeeling sightseeing

    Tiger Hill sunrise, Ghoom Monastery, Batasia Loop; after breakfast the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (closed Thursdays), tea garden, Peace Pagoda and an optional toy train joy ride.

    Overnight DarjeelingBreakfast, dinnerFull day

  10. Day 10: Darjeeling to Bagdogra / NJP

    Morning free for last-minute tea shopping, then drive down to Bagdogra or NJP (about 3 hours).

    —Breakfast~70–90 km · 3 hrs

Drive times are typical for good weather. Sightseeing order may change because of permits, weather, road work or monastery timings; nothing is skipped without telling you first.

Hotel category options

Hotel names are shared with your quote, based on availability for your dates. If a listed hotel is full, we book one of the same category and tell you before confirming.

Standard (3 star)

Clean, centrally located hotels with private bathroom, geyser and room heater on request. Good value for families who will be out sightseeing all day.

Superior (4 star)

Larger rooms, lift, in-house restaurant and better views. In Gangtok usually walking distance from MG Marg; in Darjeeling close to the Mall.

Deluxe / premium

Top properties with mountain-view rooms, central heating and a wider buffet. Best for honeymooners and senior citizens who want maximum comfort.

Food, weather and comfort notes

Ten days is long enough that food matters. We plan the trip so that you are in Gangtok and Darjeeling – the two towns with the widest choice of restaurants, including pure-vegetarian ones – on four of the nine nights. For Lachung and Pelling we brief the hotels in advance about veg, Jain or no-onion-garlic needs.

For senior citizens, every long drive is followed by a shorter day, and Gurudongmar is deliberately left out. If Lachung is closed on your dates, we replace it with an extra night in Gangtok or Ravangla.

Grand Sikkim & Kalimpong 9N/10D: common questions

Is ten days too long for Sikkim and Darjeeling?

Not if you are travelling from far away. Two days go in flights and road transfers, so ten days gives you about seven full days in the hills at a pace where parents and kids do not get exhausted.

Is Gurudongmar included?

No. We keep this tour below 12,500 ft for overnight stays and day visits, which makes it suitable for most families and seniors. Gurudongmar is part of our North Sikkim 7N/8D tour.

Can we visit Namchi Char Dham as a pilgrimage stop?

Yes, it is included on Day 8. Char Dham has replicas of Badrinath, Kedarnath, Dwarka and Jagannath Puri, and the 12 jyotirlingas, along with a large Shiva statue. There are lifts and ramps for senior citizens.

What if one of us cannot handle long drives?

Tell us at booking. We can add a night in Ravangla to split the Gangtok–Pelling day, or drop Lachung and spend that time in Gangtok.

Is this good for summer holidays with school kids?

Yes, April to June is the most popular time for this tour with families. Book 2 to 3 months ahead because Gangtok and Darjeeling hotels fill up fast in May.

Can we start from NJP after a train journey?

Yes. If you come via Kolkata by overnight train to NJP, we pick you up at the station; the drive to Kalimpong is about 2.5 hours.
