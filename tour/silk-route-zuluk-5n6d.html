Silk Route Zuluk Tour – 5 Nights / 6 Days

The Old Silk Route through East Sikkim is for travellers who prefer quiet villages and big views over crowded viewpoints. You stay in Aritar by a lake, sleep a night in Zuluk at about 10,000 ft, watch sunrise on Kanchenjunga from Thambi View Point, and cross the high Nathang valley before coming down to Gangtok and Kalimpong.

  • Best for: Friend groups, photographers, couples who like offbeat places
  • Free pickup: Bagdogra (IXB) / NJP
  • Highest point: Nathang Valley and Kupup, about 13,000–13,500 ft (driving through)

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1: NJP / Bagdogra to Aritar

    Pickup and drive to Aritar in East Sikkim, about 4 to 5 hours via Rangpo and Rorathang. Aritar has the boat-shaped Lampokhari Lake and a quiet view of the valley. Short walk to Mankhim viewpoint in the evening if you are not tired.

    Overnight AritarDinner~110 km · 4–5 hrs

  2. Day 2: Aritar to Zuluk

    The Silk Route permit is processed at Rongli on the way (we file it in advance with your ID copies). Climb through Lingtam and Padamchen to Zuluk at about 10,000 ft. Rest in the afternoon – taking it easy at this height is important. Homestay dinner, early to bed.

    Overnight ZulukBreakfast, dinner~45 km · 3–4 hrs

  3. Day 3: Thambi View Point sunrise, Nathang, Kupup, to Gangtok

    Pre-dawn drive to Thambi View Point for sunrise over Kanchenjunga with the famous Zuluk zig-zag road below you. After breakfast cross Lungthung and the wide Nathang Valley, then Kupup (Elephant) Lake and Old Baba Mandir, and descend past Tsomgo Lake to Gangtok. The exact route depends on the permit and the road on the day; if the high road is closed we return via Rongli.

    Overnight GangtokBreakfast, dinnerFull day · ~7–8 hrs

  4. Day 4: Gangtok local sightseeing

    A slow day after the high country: Enchey Monastery, Namgyal Institute of Tibetology, Do Drul Chorten, Banjhakri Falls and Tashi View Point. Evening on MG Marg.

    Overnight GangtokBreakfast, dinnerCity tour · 5 hrs

  5. Day 5: Gangtok to Kalimpong

    Drive to Kalimpong, about 3 hours. Afternoon visits to Deolo Hill, Durpin Monastery, a cactus and flower nursery, and the old Dr Graham's Homes campus.

    Overnight KalimpongBreakfast, dinner~75 km · 3 hrs

  6. Day 6: Kalimpong to NJP / Bagdogra

    Drive down to NJP station or Bagdogra airport, about 2.5 to 3 hours.

    —Breakfast~70 km · 2.5–3 hrs

Drive times are typical for good weather. Sightseeing order may change because of permits, weather, road work or monastery timings; nothing is skipped without telling you first.

Food, weather and comfort notes for South Indian travellers

Only for Indian citizens. Foreign nationals are not given permits for Zuluk and the Silk Route. Overnight at Zuluk (about 10,000 ft) is fine for most healthy adults but we do not recommend it for small children, or for anyone with heart, lung or blood pressure problems.

Food on the Silk Route is homestay cooking – rice, dal, potato and seasonal greens, eggs, chicken on request. If you are pure vegetarian or Jain, tell us; the homestay will cook separately. Carry dry snacks and ready-to-eat packs for the long day over Nathang.

Silk Route – Zuluk 5N/6D: common questions

Do we need a special permit for Zuluk and the Silk Route?

Yes. It is processed via Rongli with self-attested ID copies (Aadhaar, voter ID, passport or driving licence) and 2 passport photos per person. We file it in advance. Foreign nationals are not permitted on this route.

Is the Zuluk route safe?

The road is narrow, with more than 30 hairpin bends near Zuluk, but it is an army-maintained road and our drivers run it regularly. We never drive it at night, and in snow or heavy rain we wait or change the route.

Is the Silk Route good for senior citizens?

Only for fit seniors who are fine with simple homestays, cold nights and a long drive at high altitude. For most parents above 65 we suggest the Gangtok–Darjeeling or Darjeeling–Pelling–Gangtok tours instead.

When can we see snow on the Silk Route?

Usually from December to March around Nathang and Kupup. Heavy snowfall can temporarily close the road between Zuluk and Kupup; in that case we return the same way and visit Tsomgo from Gangtok.

Is there mobile network in Zuluk?

BSNL and Jio usually work in Zuluk village, but signal is patchy on the high road. We give you the homestay landline number so family at home can reach you.
