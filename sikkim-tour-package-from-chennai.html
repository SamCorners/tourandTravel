Most of our Chennai guests come to Sikkim for one of two reasons. Either they want to escape the May heat – when Chennai is at 40°C, Gangtok is usually somewhere between 15°C and 22°C – or they want their children and parents to see real snow for the first time. Both are very doable. The only thing that makes a Sikkim trip from Chennai different from one from Delhi or Kolkata is distance, and that is something we plan around from the first phone call.

How to travel from Chennai to Sikkim

Sikkim's practical gateway is Bagdogra airport (IXB) near Siliguri in West Bengal. From Bagdogra it is about 124 km and 4 to 5 hours by road to Gangtok, or about 3 hours to Darjeeling. Sikkim's own Pakyong airport has very limited flights that are often cancelled due to weather, so we do not build plans around it.

By flight: Chennai (MAA) to Bagdogra (IXB)

Flights from Chennai to Bagdogra are usually one-stop, connecting through Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi or Guwahati. With a sensible layover the total journey is roughly 5 to 8 hours. Airline schedules change every season, so check the current options when you book – but the rule we give every Chennai family is the same:

  • Take the earliest departure you can manage so you land in Bagdogra by about 1 pm. That gets you to Gangtok before dark. Hill roads are best driven in daylight.
  • If you can only land in the afternoon, we start your trip in Darjeeling instead of Gangtok – it is only about 3 hours from the airport – and reverse the itinerary.
  • Keep at least 2 hours for the connection if your two flights are on separate tickets, and carry snacks for children because airport food on a Kolkata layover can be limited.

By train: Chennai to New Jalpaiguri (NJP)

A few long-distance trains from Chennai to the North East stop at New Jalpaiguri, but the journey takes around two days each way. It works for college groups and families with a long vacation who enjoy train travel. A more popular middle path is to fly Chennai to Kolkata and take an overnight train such as the Darjeeling Mail from Sealdah, reaching NJP early the next morning. We pick you up at NJP station at no extra cost, whatever time your train arrives.

How many days should a Chennai family plan?

From Chennai, the first and last day of any Sikkim trip are mostly travel. That is why we rarely suggest anything shorter than 6 nights / 7 days for Chennai families. It gives you five proper days in the hills: Darjeeling for Tiger Hill and the toy train, Pelling for the closest view of Kanchenjunga, and Gangtok with Tsomgo Lake. Our Darjeeling–Pelling–Gangtok 6N/7D tour is built exactly for this.

If you only have five days off, the Gangtok & Darjeeling 5N/6D package covers the essentials without rushing. For the two-month summer vacation, many Chennai families take the Grand Sikkim 9N/10D tour, which adds Lachung, Namchi Char Dham and Kalimpong with enough rest days for grandparents.

A Tamil-speaking coordinator on call

Your driver in Sikkim will speak Nepali, Hindi and some English. That is enough for directions, but not always enough when your mother wants to explain to the hotel kitchen that she needs rice cooked soft, or when a child is unwell and you want to describe it properly. Our coordinator speaks Tamil and is a phone call away from the day you enquire until you are back at Bagdogra. They will talk to the hotel, the driver or the pharmacy on your behalf.

Food: what Chennai families can expect

Gangtok and Darjeeling are used to South Indian tourists. You will find restaurants serving idli, dosa, uttapam and South Indian thalis in both towns, and every hotel we use can make plain rice, dal and a dry vegetable. With a day's notice most will also make curd rice, lemon rice, upma or pongal for breakfast. We send your food preferences – pure vegetarian, Jain, no onion-garlic, or non-vegetarian – to each hotel before you arrive.

Two things are harder to find: good filter coffee and proper sambar. Carry your own coffee powder or decoction and a packet of sambar or rasam powder; hotel kitchens are usually happy to use it. In Lachung, Pelling and the Silk Route villages, food is simple home cooking, so ready-to-eat packs and podi are worth the suitcase space. More tips in our guide to South Indian food in Sikkim and Darjeeling.

Packing for the cold when you live in Chennai

Most Chennai households do not own real winter clothing, and you do not need to buy everything before the trip. What you should bring: thermal innerwear for everyone, woollen socks, a cap that covers the ears, gloves and comfortable closed shoes. A light jacket is enough for Gangtok in April–May. For Tsomgo Lake, heavy coats and snow boots can be hired at the lake for a small fee. If you need a proper jacket, the markets in Siliguri and on MG Marg in Gangtok sell them at reasonable prices – and we can stop on the way.

When do Chennai families usually travel?

  • April–May summer holidays: the busiest time. Pleasant weather, rhododendrons, snow still at Tsomgo in April. Book 2–3 months ahead.
  • October (Ayudha Pooja / Deepavali holidays): clear skies and the best mountain views. Our favourite month.
  • December and Pongal holidays in January: real snow and cold – zero or below at night in Darjeeling and Gangtok's higher areas. Good for adventurous families; Nathula and some high roads can close after heavy snowfall.

Permits, altitude and senior citizens

Tsomgo Lake, Nathula and North Sikkim need permits, which we apply for using scanned copies of your Aadhaar or voter ID and passport photos – see our Sikkim permit guide. For grandparents, Gangtok and Darjeeling are comfortable. We keep high-altitude places like Tsomgo to short day visits, and we do not recommend Gurudongmar Lake for anyone with heart or breathing conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a direct flight from Chennai to Bagdogra?

Direct flights between Chennai and Bagdogra are not always available; most itineraries connect through Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi or Guwahati. Check current schedules when you book. We help you choose a timing that gets you to the hills before dark.

What is the best Sikkim package for a Chennai family with grandparents?

The Darjeeling–Pelling–Gangtok 6N/7D tour or the Grand Sikkim 9N/10D tour. Both keep overnight stays at comfortable heights and include rest time after long drives.

Do you have a Tamil-speaking person to help us?

Yes. A Tamil-speaking coordinator is on call through your trip to talk to hotels, drivers or doctors for you. Drivers themselves speak Hindi, Nepali and basic English.

Will we get South Indian vegetarian food in Gangtok?

Yes. Gangtok and Darjeeling have restaurants serving idli, dosa and thalis, and hotels can make curd rice, upma and dal-rice on request. Food is simpler in Lachung and Pelling.
